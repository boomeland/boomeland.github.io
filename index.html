Sound of Valais

Ambient sounds from the Swiss Alps for sleep, focus and relaxation.

Sound of Valais is an independent YouTube channel of long, calm soundscapes inspired by the Valais, a region in the heart of the Swiss Alps: rain on vineyards, crackling fires in wooden cabins, mountain streams, alpine wind and quiet snowy nights. No music, no talking, just sound.

Sound of Valais Studio

Sound of Valais Studio is the private tool the channel owner uses to schedule uploads of the channel's own videos. It connects to YouTube through YouTube API Services, only on the Sound of Valais channel, and is not offered to anyone else. Read the privacy policy to see exactly what it accesses and how that access can be revoked.

The sounds published on the channel are created with AI and selected for each scene. Videos are labelled as altered or synthetic content on YouTube.
